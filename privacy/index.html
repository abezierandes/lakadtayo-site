Tuloy Tayo

Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

This policy explains how the Tuloy Tayo app for iPhone and Android ("Tuloy Tayo", "we", "us") handles your information. Tuloy Tayo is designed to work entirely on your device.

In short
  • We read only your daily step count, and only if you allow it.
  • Your data is stored on your phone. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • You can turn off access or delete everything at any time.

Information we access

With your permission, Tuloy Tayo reads your step count from Apple Health (HealthKit) on iPhone or Health Connect on Android. We use it to show today's progress toward your daily goal, your weekly and monthly history, and your milestones.

We do not read any other health data, such as heart rate, sleep, location or medical records. We never write data to Apple Health or Health Connect. On Android, Health Connect gives the app access to roughly the last 30 days of step data.

You can use Tuloy Tayo without granting access. You can still set goals and reminders, but no step data will be shown.

Information stored on your device

To work offline, the app keeps the following in a private database on your phone:

  • Daily step totals (one number per day, not individual readings)
  • Your daily step goals, including past goals, so your history stays accurate
  • Milestones you have reached
  • Your reminder settings and time zone
  • When step data was last refreshed

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, advertising identifiers or device identifiers.

How your information is shared

It isn't. Tuloy Tayo has no servers and does not send your information over the internet. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising, marketing or data brokering.

Health data obtained through Apple HealthKit is used only to provide the app's features and is never shared with third parties.

Tuloy Tayo's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Reminders

If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled and delivered by your phone. No reminder data leaves your device. We ask for notification permission only when you choose to turn reminders on.

Backups

On iPhone, app data may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, depending on your backup settings. These backups are managed by Apple and you, not by us. On Android, Tuloy Tayo's data is excluded from device backups.

Your choices and control

  • Turn off step access: on iPhone, open the Health app, then your profile, then Apps, then Tuloy Tayo. On Android, open Health Connect, then App permissions, then Tuloy Tayo.
  • Delete your data: in the app, go to Profile, then Delete all local data. This removes your goals, history, milestones and reminders from the device. Uninstalling the app also deletes them. Data in Apple Health or Health Connect is not affected.
  • Turn off reminders: in the app under Profile, or in your phone's notification settings.

Data retention

Information stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. We have no copy to retain or delete.

Children

Tuloy Tayo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Not medical advice

Tuloy Tayo is a habit and wellness app. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Talk to a healthcare professional before starting a new exercise routine if you have health concerns.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
