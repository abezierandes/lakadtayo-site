Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

This policy explains how the Lakad Tayo app for iPhone and Android ("Lakad Tayo", "we", "us") handles your information. Lakad Tayo works on your phone first. An account is optional and only needed for buddies and buddy challenges.

In short
  • We read your daily steps, and distance and active energy if you allow them, from Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Your step history, goals and achievements stay on your phone.
  • If you create an account and share with buddies, they see a daily summary of your progress. Exact step counts only if you allow it.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • You can turn off access, stop sharing, or delete your account and data at any time, in the app.

Health data we read

With your permission, Lakad Tayo reads your daily step count from Apple Health (HealthKit) on iPhone or Health Connect on Android. If you also turn them on, it reads your daily walking and running distance and active energy. We use this to show today's progress, your history, streaks, achievements and challenges.

We do not read any other health data, such as heart rate, sleep, location or medical records, and we never write data to Apple Health or Health Connect. You can use Lakad Tayo without granting access; no step data will be shown.

Information stored on your phone

To work offline, the app keeps the following in a private database on your phone:

  • Daily totals: steps, and distance and active energy if allowed (one number per day, not individual readings)
  • Your daily step goals, including past goals, so your history stays accurate
  • Achievements and solo challenges you have started or completed
  • Reminder settings and your time zone

None of this leaves your phone unless you use the account features below.

Optional account: buddies and challenges

To add buddies or join buddy challenges you can create an account by signing in with your email address and a one-time code. If you do, we store on our servers:

  • Account details: your email address, a user ID, and the username and display name you choose.
  • Connections: your buddies, buddy requests, invite codes, coach links and cheers.
  • Notification token: if you allow notifications for buddy requests on iPhone, the push token your phone gives us so Apple can deliver them. It is deleted when you sign out, when you delete your account, or when Apple tells us it is no longer valid.
  • Challenges: buddy challenges you create, are invited to or join.
  • Blocks and reports: people you block, and reports you send about another user (the reason, and their username and display name at that time). Reports are reviewed by us and are never shown to the person reported.
  • Daily summaries, only while someone can see them (you share with a buddy or coach, or you are in a buddy challenge): for each of the last 30 days, the date, your goal, whether you reached it, were active or had no data, and your step count.

Who can see what:

  • Buddies and coaches you share with see your daily summary: goal reached, active or no data, and your goal.
  • They see your exact step counts only if you turn on "Share step counts" for them. This is enforced on our servers.
  • Members of a buddy challenge you join see your daily step totals for that challenge's dates. The app tells you this before you create or join one.
  • Other people can find you only by typing your exact username. There is no public directory or search.

When you stop sharing with everyone and are not in a running challenge, your daily summaries are deleted from our servers.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the account features. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase hosts the account database and sign-in, in Singapore (Amazon Web Services, ap-southeast-1).
  • Our email provider delivers your sign-in codes. The email contains only the code.
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers buddy request notifications on iPhone. It receives your notification token and the notification's text.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website, including the page that invite links open. An invite link contains only the invite code. Like any website host, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses.

We do not sell, rent or share your information for advertising, marketing or data brokering. Health data obtained through Apple HealthKit is used only to provide the app's features, and is never used for advertising or shared with third parties for those purposes. Lakad Tayo's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Notifications

Daily reminders and the weekly summary are scheduled and delivered by your phone. If you have an account and allow it, we also send a notification when someone sends you a buddy request or accepts yours; it shows their display name and username. Notifications never include your step counts. We ask for notification permission only when you turn on a reminder or the weekly summary, or after you send your first buddy request. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's Settings.

Security

Data sent to our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access rules on the server ensure people only see what you have chosen to share. Your sign-in session is stored in your phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone).

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off health access: on iPhone, open the Health app, then your profile, then Apps, then Lakad Tayo. On Android, open Health Connect, then App permissions, then Lakad Tayo.
  • Stop sharing with anyone, or remove a buddy, on the Buddies tab. Leave a challenge from its screen.
  • Block or report someone from their buddy profile or their buddy request. Blocking ends all sharing between you and stops them finding you or sending requests. Unblock on the Buddies tab.
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Profile, then Account, then Delete account. This deletes your account and everything stored with it on our servers, immediately.
  • Delete data on your phone: Profile, then Delete all local data, or uninstall the app. Data in Apple Health or Health Connect is not affected.

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and similar laws, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to its processing. Most of this you can do directly in the app; for anything else, contact us below.

Data retention

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Account data stays until you delete your account. Daily summaries are kept only while someone can see them, and for at most the last 30 days.

Backups

On iPhone, the app's on-device data may be included in your iCloud or computer backups, depending on your settings. These backups are managed by Apple and you, not by us.

Children

Lakad Tayo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Not medical advice

Lakad Tayo is a habit and wellness app. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Suggested goals are starting points, not prescriptions. Talk to a healthcare professional before starting a new exercise routine if you have health concerns.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
