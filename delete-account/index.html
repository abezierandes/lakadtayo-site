Delete your Lakad Tayo account

An account is optional in Lakad Tayo and is only used for buddies and buddy challenges. You can delete it, and everything stored with it on our servers, at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Lakad Tayo and go to Profile.
  2. Under Your account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email lakadtayo.app@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Lakad Tayo account". We delete the account within 7 days and reply when it's done. If the email comes from a different address, we'll ask you to confirm the request from your sign-in address first.

What is deleted

  • Your email address, user ID, username and display name.
  • Your buddies, buddy requests, invite codes, coach links and cheers.
  • Buddy challenges you joined or created, and your daily summaries.
  • People you blocked, and notification tokens for your devices.

Reports you sent about other users are kept so we can act on them, but no longer linked to you. Nothing is kept in backups beyond our hosting provider's standard short-term database backups, which expire on their own.

Delete some data and keep your account

  • Shared daily summaries: on the Buddies tab, stop sharing with each buddy or coach and leave your challenges. When no one can see your progress any more, your daily summaries are deleted from our servers right away.
  • A buddy or coach link: open them on the Buddies tab and remove them. Pending buddy requests can be cancelled or declined there too.
  • A challenge: open it and tap Leave challenge.
  • Notifications: turning notifications off in your phone's Settings stops pushes; signing out removes this phone's notification token from our servers.
  • Anything else: email lakadtayo.app@gmail.com from your sign-in address and say what to delete. We do it within 7 days.

Data on your phone

Your step history, goals and achievements are stored only on your phone and are not part of your account. To remove them, go to Profile → Delete all local data, or uninstall the app. Data in Apple Health or Health Connect is not affected.

See the privacy policy for details on what Lakad Tayo stores and why.
