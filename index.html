Lakad Tayo

Every step is better together.

A little walking goes a long way.

Build a walking habit one achievable day at a time, and bring your buddies along.

Lakad Tayo is a walking companion for iPhone and Android. Pick a daily step goal that feels doable, watch your progress and streaks, take on beginner-friendly challenges, and cheer each other on with buddies.

Your step history stays on your phone, and you choose what to share. Read our privacy policy.
